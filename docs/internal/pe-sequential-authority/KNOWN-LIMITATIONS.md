# Known limitations

INTERNAL_CANDIDATE | EVALUATE_ONLY | NOT_HOST_ENFORCEMENT | NOT_SHIPPED | NO_KERNEL | NO_ENROLL | NO_CREDENTIAL_MATERIAL | NO_STABLE_SCHEMA

Audience: INTERNAL_RESTRICTED

Evidence tier: `UNSET`. Customer validation: `UNSET`.

This candidate decides envelopes it is given. It does not establish who the customer root is. A caller who can supply a root envelope is outside the check. Identity remains an opaque id. No identity provider is selected.

The ledger is an input. A discarded ledger starts counters at zero. A ledger that omits family history can hide a composition the omitted steps would have denied. Binding that ledger to an append-only host record is outside this package. Doctrine residual on evidence rewrite stays in the reference-monitor file. This package does not implement it.

`revoke` applies a transition. Authorization to revoke is a separate `evaluate` step for `revoke_grant` or root `revoke_root`. Calling `revoke` by itself records the mark. It is the state change a caller applies after an allow decision.

Redelegation stays forbidden under the EG-D1 default. EG-D1 through EG-D10 stay unresolved. The candidate does not choose a company-wide duration. Callers supply `not_before`, `not_after`, and ceilings. Those numbers are not a Founder decision.

The composition table is closed: observe/label, propose/record, exercise/name, ceiling raises, second effects, and unbound pairs. Other sequences are limited by membership and ceilings. The table is not a general planner.

Credential and destination counters inside one ledger are keyed by id. Two envelopes that reuse one id share that counter. Tenant, fleet, node, and workload keys are shared the same way inside one ledger.

Parser maps reject more than 10000 entries. That bound fails closed. It is not a product quota.

Reference-monitor rows RM-02 and RM-09 stay partial and skip in the cited doctrine. Unit tests in this repository do not change those rows. They are not a stranger-host pass, not a Phantom-Box prove, and not EG-SP-1 through EG-SP-5.

Host attachment, kernel load, enroll, and customer deploy stay closed. `doctrine_present` remains false.
