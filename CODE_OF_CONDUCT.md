# Code of Conduct

People who take part in issues, pull requests, and discussions on this repository are expected to stay respectful, specific, and careful with other people's data.

## Expected behavior

- Keep the discussion on the work.
- Ask when a requirement is unclear.
- Leave secrets, customer data, prompts, and completions out of issues, pull requests, and logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Posting unfixed vulnerability details in public. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting

Send conduct concerns to hello@vantio.ai.

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, or block further participation, when behavior breaks this policy. We acknowledge reports when we can. We do not promise a response time.
